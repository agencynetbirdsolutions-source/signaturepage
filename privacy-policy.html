Privacy Policy

How this website handles information when you visit or reach out to us.

Information We Collect Through This Website

This website provides promotional information and includes a contact option through WhatsApp. The website does not currently provide an enquiry form, user account registration, direct payment facility, or personal visitor database.

Google Ads conversion tracking may be used to measure website visits and interactions such as clicks on the WhatsApp contact link. This information may help us understand how visitors interact with our promotional content.

Website Hosting and Fonts

When you visit this website your browser may automatically send certain technical information to the website host. This may include your IP address, browser related information, request details, and other technical data required to load and secure the website.

The website also uses the Inter typeface through Google Fonts. When the font is loaded your browser may send the necessary request information to Google in order to provide the typeface.

WhatsApp Enquiries

When you select the WhatsApp contact option or promotional link you may be redirected through our WhatsApp contact link to WhatsApp.

WhatsApp and other third party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. If you send a message through WhatsApp, the message content and contact information associated with your WhatsApp account may become available to the recipient.

For your security, please avoid sharing passwords, OTPs, payment credentials, identity documents, banking information, or other sensitive personal information in an initial enquiry.

Third Party Services

This website may use third party services for advertising measurement, website hosting, fonts, and communication. These providers may process certain technical or interaction related information according to their respective privacy policies.

We do not control the privacy practices, data retention policies, or processing activities of third party providers.

Privacy Questions

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to ask how an enquiry is handled, you can contact the team through the WhatsApp contact link.

Information processed or retained by third party services is governed by the privacy policies and terms of those respective providers.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website or its services. Any revised version will be published on this page.
